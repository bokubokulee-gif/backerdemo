HOW BACKER MODELS A LAUNCH

Understand the result.
Then change the inputs.

  1. Brief the campaign

    Your product, market, budget, creators and creative approach become editable inputs. Attachments are read in your browser. Original files are not uploaded; extracted text is sent to the simulation service when you simulate.

  2. Model individual posts

    Each proposed post has a hook, storyboard, CTA, spend and a funnel from impressions to a defined conversion.

  3. Compare possible outcomes

    1,200 seeded paths vary attention and funnel assumptions. The median is a central scenario; the 10th–90th range is model spread, not measured forecast accuracy.

What this demo knows

The example campaigns use editable product, audience and creative choices. Creator identities and linked reference posts are evidence. Fees, reach, click rates, conversion rates and future comments are explicit illustrative assumptions unless labeled otherwise.

Public likes or comments do not establish paid conversions. Production accuracy needs consented first-party attribution and held-out campaign backtesting. This demo has not been calibrated to predict real launches.

Calculation and AI routing

CPM = allocated spend ÷ modeled impressions × 1,000. Engagements = likes + comments + shares + saves. Conversions = modeled clicks × conversion rate. Totals are quantiled from joint paths; median rows need not sum exactly.

The scenario service recognizes explicit campaign terms and structured fields. Optional TypeSafe Jev routing selects typed campaign options, never outcome probabilities. It runs only when a server key is configured and you opt in.
